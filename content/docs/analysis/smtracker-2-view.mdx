---
title: "SMTracker 2.0 view"
description: "SMTracker 2.0's tabs and methods on the conditions you have loaded."
---

**SMTracker 2.0 view**, the last row under the analyses in the sidebar, opens a separate window that looks and computes like SMTracker 2.0: its tabs **Import**, **SLA**, **MSD**, **Clustering**, **GMM**, **SQD**, **APPD** and **SDA**, with SMTracker 2.0's settings, plots, tables and words, on the conditions this window has loaded. Use it to see what SMTracker 2.0 would have said about the same data.

Each tab works as it did in SMTracker 2.0: pick the settings on the left and click **Run & Plot**. Click a track in the **Import** tab's image to open the **Track Explorer**. **File > Print figures** saves the current tab's plots as 300 dpi PNGs, each name starting with `legacy_`.

<Callout type="note">
The numbers are SMTracker 2.0's, including its pooled statistics and its error bars, and they are never shown beside SMTracker 3's. For results, use the analyses in this window.
</Callout>
