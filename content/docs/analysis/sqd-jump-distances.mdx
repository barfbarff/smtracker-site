---
title: "SQD: Jump distances"
description: "The distribution of jump distances, with held-out validation."
---

This page shows each condition's jump-distance histogram against the 1-, 2- and 3-population fits (4 on request), with residuals and a fit-quality table. The table gives D, the fractions, ΔBIC, the largest ECDF gap and a held-out test: each fit is made without one replicate (or one group of cells) and scored on what it left out. The number of populations that wins the held-out test is the one the data support.

**Run** on this page asks for:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Conditions** | Which conditions to fit. | All |
| **Fit up to** | **3 populations**, or **4 populations** (about four times slower). | 3 |
| **Held out** | What the held-out test leaves out: **Replicates** or **Cells**. | Replicates |

The fits use the Time lag chosen in the Compute dialog.

**The card for one condition**

- **Show** has one pill per fit. Click a pill to draw or remove that fit; at least one stays on. A card starts with the count that BIC prefers. One fit is drawn alone in black; several are drawn in their own colors.
- **Components** (selected to start) shows each fit's populations.
- The Pearson and ECDF residual plots beside the histogram show the same fits. Bins beyond ±2 are red.
- **Y axis** sets the unit: **Probability** (default; the bars sum to 1, as in SMTracker 2 and Spot-On), **Counts** (jumps per bin) or **Density** (per nm). Bins are 10 nm wide and only affect how jumps are drawn; the fits use the jumps themselves.
- **Axes** accepts a range for x (nm) and y. An empty field means automatic. A typed range stays until you press **Auto**, and changing the y unit clears the y range.
- **Same y scale** (over the cards and in All conditions) puts every plot of one unit on the largest range among them; the button then reads **Own y scales**. Use Probability or Density for this, since counts from different numbers of jumps do not compare.
- Drag a histogram's key to move it; a double-click returns it to its corner. A card's **Export** keeps its position.

**All conditions**

The switch at the top right shows every condition's histogram on one screen, on one jump-distance axis.

- **Show for every condition** sets every card's pills at once, and its **Components** box sets every card's.
- **Residuals**: select **Pearson** or **ECDF** (both off to start) to draw that residual plot under every histogram.
- **Plots**: **One plot per condition**, or **One plot per fit**, which gives each fit of each condition its own plot, alone with its components. With one plot per fit, **Conditions down** puts each condition in a row and **Conditions across** swaps rows and columns.
- **Columns** (one plot per condition) sets how many plots stand across; **Auto** fits as many as the page holds.
- **Size** (S, M, L) makes the plots larger. Double-click a plot to spread it across the page.
- **Y axis**, **Same y scale** and **Axes…** apply to every plot; a plot's own Axes wins.

**Export**

A card's **Export** and **Export all…** (the whole page as one figure) open the Export dialog. Its boxes:

| Box | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fit** | The fits drawn; starts on the card's. On Export all, **Cards** keeps each card's own. |
| **Show** | **Components**, **Pearson residuals**, **ECDF residuals**. On a card's figure both residual boxes start selected; on Export all they follow the Residuals boxes. Unticking one removes that plot and its numbers from the figure and source data. |
| **Y unit** | Starts on the card's unit. |
| **Axes** | The figure starts on the card's typed ranges. |
| **Same y scale** | Gives every panel of Export all one y range. |
| **Grid** | Export all only: rows and columns, starting on the page's Columns choice. |

**Add to composer** keeps these choices in the panel.

**Signed jumps** (the strip's third page, with its own **Run**) is a cross-check on a different observable. Each jump is measured along one axis at a time, so a jump to the left is negative, and the jumps are fitted with mixtures of 1, 2 and 3 normal distributions (D = σ²/2τ). Switch between **Along the cell**, **Across the cell** and **Camera x, y**, and between 1, 2 and 3 populations, without refitting. A cell is about 1 µm wide, so a fast molecule's jumps across it are cut short: its across D reads lower than its along D, and the table gives the ratio. On the camera axes the SQD fit of the same jumps appears beside it. The spread is the SD over replicates. Do not average these fractions with the SQD ones; it is the same data measured another way. It uses the Compute dialog's Time lag. Each card's **Export** saves the axis shown; the table across conditions has its own Export. There are no residual plots.
