## SMTracker 3.0.0

*Released 27 November 2026.* [Download](https://github.com/longestgiraffe/smtracker/releases/tag/v3.0.0)

SMTracker 3 is a new program, written in Python. It no longer needs MATLAB, and it comes with an installer for Windows and for Macs with Apple silicon ([Install and open SMTracker](/docs/install)).

- **From raw movies to tracks.** Crops Nikon `.nd2`, MetaMorph `.stk` and TIFF movies to their usable frames, saves the calibration, and runs TrackMate (Fiji) or u-track (MATLAB) for one condition or many folders overnight ([Image preparation + Tracking](/docs/tracking)).
- **Cell outlines.** Finds cells with a classical segmenter or, after an optional download of PyTorch and Cellpose, with Cellpose-SAM; corrects them in an outline editor; and trains your own model ([Cell outlines](/docs/cell-outlines)).
- **Tracks from other tools.** Reads trackpy, MOSAIC ParticleTracker, TrackIt, Spot-On, evalSPT/SLIMfast and TrackMate CSV tables, or any table whose columns and units you describe ([Tracks from other tools](/docs/other-tools)).
- **Error bars over replicates.** Every fraction gets its spread over independent replicates, and conditions are compared with Welch's test, corrected with Holm or Benjamini-Hochberg ([How replicates are counted](/docs/replicates)).
- **The data choose the number of populations**, by BIC, from many random starting points.
- **Analyses:** jump distances (SQD), apparent diffusion, MSD, a hidden Markov model with switching rates, populations over time after an addition, spatial maps, clustering, and a QC page that checks whether conditions can be compared fairly ([Analysis](/docs/analysis)).
- **Localization error per spot**, from a calibration measured on immobile molecules.
- **Simulated data.** Builds data sets with known populations, diffusion and switching, to compare SMTracker's results with the truth.
- **Figures for print**, each with its source data as `.xlsx` and `.csv` ([Figures](/docs/analysis/figures)).
- **SMTracker 2.0 view.** SMTracker 2.0's eight tabs, computed its way, on the conditions you have loaded ([SMTracker 2.0 view](/docs/analysis/smtracker-2-view)).
- **Installer and updates.** Installs without administrator rights, offers the segmentation pack and Fiji, and looks once a day for a newer release, which it installs only when you click ([Update SMTracker](/docs/install#update-smtracker)).
- **Report on GitHub.** Report a problem saves the log as a zip and opens a new report on GitHub with the version and the end of the log filled in; nothing is sent until you submit it.
- **Fixes to SMTracker 2.0:** fraction error bars assigned to the wrong condition, a crash with one population, a limit of seven conditions, and fits that failed without saying so.

## SMTracker 2.0

*2021*

The MATLAB version, described in Oviedo-Bocanegra et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 49, e112 (2021), [doi:10.1093/nar/gkab696](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkab696). It needs MATLAB R2014b.

## SMTracker 1.0

*2018*

The first version, described in Rösch et al., *Scientific Reports* (2018).
