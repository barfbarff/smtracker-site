## Callout

```mdx
<Callout type="note | app | check | refused">…</Callout>
```

<Callout type="note">The folders above a condition carry no meaning: rename or move them freely.</Callout>
<Callout type="app">The moon icon at the bottom of the analysis window switches to dark, and is remembered.</Callout>
<Callout type="check">On the same movies u-track and TrackMate differ by about 1 point in the slow fraction. Mixing them also compares trackers.</Callout>
<Callout type="refused">A table whose units are unknown is not read. Add `track_tables/format.json` with its units and open the folder again.</Callout>
